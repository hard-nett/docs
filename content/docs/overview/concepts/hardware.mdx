title: Hardware
---

Hardware is the physical computers,components, or devices. These are the tangible interfaces for Terp Network and the external world. Hardware devices are able to recieve and transmit info on Terp Network. In a perfect world, everyon interacting and contributing to Terp Netowrk is doing so usig software they run on their own hardware they own. Its important to include hardware because this is what makes up the actual layer of trust, not just includeing nodes running the blockchain but sensors, relayers, broadcasting networks, energy infra all combine to make it posisble to have a neutral verifiacation layer like Terp Network.

# Network Genesis

Genesis is the birth of a blockchain — the first block, the initial state, and the distribution of tokens. Terp Network's genesis involved a multi-phase launch designed to maximize fair distribution and community participation.

## Genesis Events

| Event | Description | Status |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Chain start | First block of `morocco-1` | Complete |
| Airdrop | Token distribution to community members | Complete |
| Headstash | Headstash NFT for genesis participants | Complete |
| OG Challenge | Proof-of-work puzzles for early adopters | Complete |
| Scavenger Hunt | Cryptographic treasure hunt | Complete |

## Airdrop Distribution

The genesis airdrop distributed `uthiol` to participants who contributed to the ecosystem, including:

- Stakers on partner chains
- Community members
- Open source contributors
- Early testnet participants

> **Airdrop distribution chart scaffold** — a pie chart or bar chart showing the distribution of genesis tokens by category (community, contributors, stakers, reserves, etc.).

## Headstash

Headstash is a genesis NFT collection commemorating early participants. Holding a Headstash NFT may entitle holders to:

- Governance participation in certain DAOs
- Protocol fee sharing
- Community recognition

See the [Headstash guide](/docs/overview/concepts/genesis/headstash) for details.

## OG Challenge

The OG Challenge was a proof-of-work competition where participants solved cryptographic puzzles to earn genesis recognition. Three progressively harder challenges tested:

1. Hash preimage finding
2. Merkle tree construction
3. Signature verification

See the [OG Challenge guide](/docs/overview/concepts/genesis/og-challenge) for the full challenge specifications and solutions.

## Scavenger Hunt

An extension of the OG challenge, the scavenger hunt required participants to:
- Find hidden clues across multiple platforms
- Decode cryptographic messages
- Submit proofs on-chain

See the [Scavenger Hunt guide](/docs/overview/concepts/genesis/og-challenge/scavenger-hunt) for details.

## Genesis Constitution

Terp Network launched with a genesis constitution outlining the foundational principles and governance framework. See the [Constitution](/docs/overview/concepts/genesis/constitution) for the full text.
